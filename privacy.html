Privacy Policy

Application: Cowork-MCP  ·  Last updated: 14 September 2026

Cowork-MCP (“the Application”) is a personal application operated by the owner of enlumineadvisory.com (“the Operator”) for the Operator’s own use. This policy explains how the Application handles data obtained through Google APIs.

1. Scope of this policy

The Application is used solely by the Operator to access and manage the Operator’s own Google account data through automated tools. It is not offered as a service to the public, does not create accounts for other users, and does not collect data from anyone other than the Operator.

2. Information the Application accesses

When authorized by the Operator, the Application accesses data from the Operator’s Google account according to the permissions (scopes) granted at sign-in. Depending on those scopes, this may include:

  • Gmail messages, drafts, and associated metadata
  • Google Calendar events and details
  • Google Drive files and metadata

The Application accesses only the data needed to perform the actions the Operator requests.

3. How the information is used

Accessed data is used exclusively to carry out tasks the Operator initiates — for example, reading, searching, drafting, organizing, or summarizing the Operator’s own email, calendar, or files. The Application does not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the Operator’s requests.

4. Processing and disclosure to third parties

To fulfill requested tasks, the Application transmits relevant data to the AI assistant service it integrates with (Anthropic’s Claude) for processing. Apart from Google (as the source of the data) and that AI service (as the processor that completes the Operator’s requests), the Application does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with any third party. Data is processed only as needed to complete a request.

5. Data storage and retention

The Application stores only the OAuth credentials required to maintain access to the Operator’s Google account, on infrastructure controlled by the Operator. It does not maintain a separate long-term database of the Operator’s Google content. Data retrieved to satisfy a request is not retained beyond what is necessary for the Application to function.

6. Limited Use disclosure

Cowork-MCP’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Data protection

Access is protected by Google’s OAuth 2.0 authorization and by the Operator’s own account security. Authorization can be revoked at any time by the Operator from their Google Account permissions page.
